# 🧾 Receipt Hunters Gallery

## Load-bearing evidence, stupid jokes, glorious failures, and the receipts that survived them

The Receipt Hunters Gallery is the public visual-evidence wing of AI Rumble.

The joke may be ridiculous. **The provenance cannot be.**

## Classes

- **🧾 RECEIPT** — actual evidence attached to a claim, build, test, or event.
- **🔩 LOAD-BEARING RECEIPT** — evidence participating in an acceptance or audit chain.
- **🤣 LOAD-BEARING JOKE** — a joke image that also carries real project/provenance information.
- **☠️ FAILURE RECEIPT** — evidence of a failure worth preserving instead of hiding.
- **🏆 HUNTER'S TROPHY** — historically interesting community receipt.

## Minimum record

A gallery item should preserve, when applicable:

```text
title
class
image/artifact
contributor
date
project/job
caption
SHA-256
source/provenance
claim_supported
claim_not_supported
related_receipts
status
```

A funny image is not automatically proof of the claim printed on it.

A receipt establishes only what its underlying evidence actually supports.

## Community submissions

Community submissions are welcome.

Do not submit secrets, credentials, private personal information, material you do not have the right to publish, or another person's private project data.

Suggested layout:

```text
gallery/receipt-hunters/
├── README.md
├── submissions/
├── accepted/
├── failures/
├── trophies/
└── index/
```

## Hunter's Rule

> Bring the artifact, the evidence, and the receipt.

If the receipt is also funny, even better.
